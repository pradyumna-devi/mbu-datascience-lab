**Curriculum Standard**: Official Laboratory Practical

## Overview
Customer Personality Analysis segments a company's customer database based on income, spending score, and purchasing behaviors. Rather than spending capital marketing universally, the business models customer segments to deliver tailored promotions, optimize inventory, and improve retention.

### Tech Stack & Key Concepts
- **Unsupervised Learning**
- **K-Means Clustering**
- **Elbow Method & Within-Cluster Sum of Squares (Inertia)**
- **Silhouette Validation Analysis**
- **StandardScaler Feature Normalization**
- **Customer Behavioral Archetypes**
- **Pandas & Seaborn**
